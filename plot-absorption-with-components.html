Absorption With Components

The plot below shows atmospheric sound absorption versus frequency for a single temperature, pressure, and relative humidity. Adjust the temperature, pressure, and humidity with the controls above the plot.

The plot includes separate lines for the total absorption and absorption due to oxygen relaxation, nitrogen relaxation, and other processes. The total and component absorptions are computed as described in [1], and saturation vapor pressure and absolute humidity as described in Appendix B of [2]. Other useful references include [3] and [4].

The plot was inspired by a similar interactive plot created by David Russell of the Pennsylvania State University Graduate Program in Acoustics.
