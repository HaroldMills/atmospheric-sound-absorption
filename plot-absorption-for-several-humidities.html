Absorption for Several Humidities

The plot below shows atmospheric sound absorption versus frequency for a single temperature and pressure and several relative humidities. Adjust the temperature and pressure with the controls above the plot.

For a temperature of 68°F and a pressure of 1 atm (the defaults), the plot reproduces Figure B.1 of Appendix B of [1]. The same plot appears as Figure 1 of [2].

References
